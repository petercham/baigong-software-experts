# R3 标准/知识体系如何处理「多对象 × 多过程」

**结论：本轮所核八家中，没有一家把「对象类型」当主结构轴。** 除 PMBOK 6 走全矩阵外，其余七家的主结构轴都选"变化慢、可判定、与组织无关"的那一维——过程/阶段、关注点、能力/实践、用户需求、严格度；对象类型只降级为**命名/标识（buf）、第二轴索引（TOGAF／CMMI）、可裁剪的交付物目录（TOGAF）**——三者出处不同、不可互相顶替——或干脆是另一种东西（ontology 分类学），并普遍附一句"不要求你按我的结构组织你的文件"。唯一真按矩阵切的是 PMBOK 第 6 版（5 过程组 × 10 知识领域 = 49 格），而它在第 7 版**主动放弃**了矩阵；**"唯一"仅限本轮所核八家，非穷举业界**。

## 一、逐个标准（切分维度 ＋ 官方原话 ＋ URL ＋ 它放弃了哪个维度）

### 1. ISO 9001:2015（已废止，2026-09-16 被 ISO 9001:2026 取代）
- **切分维度**：按管理体系要素/过程——4 组织环境／5 领导／6 策划／7 支持／8 运行／9 绩效评价／10 改进，骨架来自 Annex SL 高阶结构，按 PDCA 排布。不按部门、不按对象类型、不按文件类型。
- **官方原话**：ISO 官网新闻（引 TC176/SC2 主席 Nigel Croft）"The 2015 version adopts a new high-level structure for ISO management systems standards – based on Annex SL…"；"Annex SL provides identical structure, text and common terms and definitions for all future ISO management system standards (MSSs), giving each a similar 'look, touch and feel'"；"The latest version obliges each organization to think about its own particular circumstances, rather than prescribing a 'recipe' for how to design the quality management system."
- **过程方法口径**（ISO 官方白皮书）"The process approach can be applied to any organization and any management system regardless of type, size or complexity."（"regardless of type"＝对象类型被明确排除在切分依据之外）
- **它放弃了哪个维度（最关键）**：9001:2015 §0.1 明文——"It is not the intent of this International Standard to imply the need for: — uniformity in the structure of different quality management systems; — **alignment of documentation to the clause structure of this International Standard**; — the use of the specific terminology of this International Standard within the organization." 即**标准条款轴 ≠ 你的文件轴**，且是明文不许对齐。CQI/IRCA 官方简报复述："Organizations do not need to… RENUMBER or rename existing QMS documentation to correspond to the new clause references."
- **"文件化信息"怎么分布**：不设独立一卷、不设文档清单。7.5 只规定"如何控制"（标识、格式、评审批准、分发、保管），"要哪些文件"由 7.5.1(b) 交给组织自定；其余要求**散落在 4–10 各条**（5.2 质量方针、8.5.1 生产/服务提供控制、9.2 内审、10.2 不合格与纠正…）。2015 版还删掉了质量手册与 6 个强制程序文件；2026 版延续"No new mandatory documents… still does not require a quality manual"。
- URL：https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html ；https://www.iso.org/standard/9001 ；https://www.iso.org/iso/iso9001_2015_process_approach.pdf ；https://www.iso.org/es/contents/news/2015/11/Ref2014.html

### 2. ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022（架构描述）
- **切分维度**：按**关注点（concern）→ viewpoint**。"one viewpoint 管一类 view"，标准只规定 concern 与 view 的对应关系必须写明。
- **官方原话**（标准编辑维护的官方概念模型页）"An Architecture Viewpoint is a set of conventions for constructing, interpreting, using and analyzing one type of Architecture View. A viewpoint includes Model Kinds, viewpoint languages and notations, modeling methods and analytic techniques to frame a specific set of Concerns."；"A Concern is any interest in the system. The term derives from the phrase 'separation of concerns' as originally coined by Edsgar Dijkstra."
- **它放弃了哪两个维度**：
  - 对象类型轴：2022 版把主体从 System of Interest 泛化成 Entity of Interest，一次覆盖 "software, systems, enterprises, systems of systems, families of systems, products (goods or services), product lines, service lines, technologies and business domains"，紧接着声明 "It does not specify requirements for any entity of interest or its environment." ＋ "Architectures are not the subject of this document."
  - 过程轴："This document does not specify the processes, architecting methods, models, notations, techniques or tools by which an AD is created, utilized or managed."；亦 "does not specify any format or media for recording an AD."
- **为什么按关注点而不按对象**：要服务的是多利益相关方的不同关切，而实体清单无法穷举——42010 干脆**不给固定的 viewpoint 集**（编辑 Hilliard 自述："We 'invented' viewpoints because we couldn't pick one set"），viewpoint 由框架/组织自定义，标准只要求 concern↔view 的对应可追溯，并用 correspondence / correspondence rule 保跨视图一致。第 2 版新增 Stakeholder Perspectives 给 concern 分组。
- URL：https://www.iso.org/standard/74393.html ；http://www.iso-architecture.org/42010/cm/

### 3. CMMI
- **切分维度**：按**过程域/实践域（process area / practice area）**，第二轴是成熟度等级 ML1–5。
- **官方原话**："A process area is a cluster of related practices in an area that, when implemented collectively, satisfies a set of goals considered important for making improvement in that area."（CMMI-DEV v1.3 词汇表）
- **组织法**：22 个 PA 正文按字母序平铺——"The 22 process areas are presented in alphabetical order by acronym"，再给两种可选分组（staged 按成熟度／continuous 按类别）。V3.0 改为 Category(Doing/Managing/Enabling/Improving) → Capability Area → Practice Area。
- **它放弃了哪个维度**：对象/工作产品轴。work product 只是"过程的有用结果（文件、文档、产品、服务、发票…）"；典型工作产品是 **informative 且可替换**——"These examples are called 'typical work products' because there are often other work products that are just as effective, but are not listed." 即工作产品清单不许当判据。
- **为什么按过程**：要评估的是"能力是否被制度化"，判据必须挂在"做什么"上才跨项目、跨产品可比；对象清单随产品变，不可比。
- URL：https://insights.sei.cmu.edu/documents/853/2010_005_001_15287.pdf ；https://cmmiinstitute.com/getattachment/3cc3e8d1-72a1-4f6d-b1da-c5df8c9ad87d/CMMI-Adoption-Guidance.pdf

### 4. TOGAF（ADM）
- **切分维度**：主轴按**阶段**（Preliminary、A–H、贯穿全程的 Requirements Management），每阶段规定输入/步骤/输出；第二轴是 **Architecture Content Framework**（deliverable / artifact / building block），只承担"内容分类与归档"。
- **官方原话**：ADM = "a tested and repeatable process for developing architectures"（TOGAF 9.2 §5）。内容三分——"A deliverable is a work product that is contractually specified and in turn formally reviewed, agreed, and signed off by the stakeholders"／"An artifact is an architectural work product that describes an aspect of the architecture… classified as catalogs (lists of things), matrices (showing relationships between things), and diagrams (pictures of things)"／"A building block represents a (potentially re-usable) component of enterprise capability that can be combined with other building blocks to deliver architectures and solutions"。
- **ABB 与 SBB 分工**（Open Group 官方会议页）"Architecture Building Blocks (ABBs) are definitions of requirements and functionality, and Solution Building Blocks (SBBs) are parts of the actual proposed solution. These are defined by an iterative process in the TOGAF ADM." → 交付物不是一个目录层，而是**挂在阶段上的产出**。
- **它放弃了哪个维度**：固定的对象/交付物清单。官方定位："The TOGAF Standard is designed for the dichotomy of common universal concepts and variable detailed configuration"，分 Fundamental Content 与 Series Guides；ADM "does not dictate scope"。交付物清单是**可裁剪的第二轴**，不是骨架。
- URL：https://www.opengroup.org/togaf ；https://archive.opengroup.org/public/member/proceedings/q113/27TO.htm

### 5. ITIL 4
- **切分维度**：按**实践（practice）**，34 个实践分三类（general / service / technical management）；第二轴是**服务价值链 6 活动**（Plan／Improve／Engage／Design & Transition／Obtain/Build／Deliver & Support）＋四维（组织与人员、信息与技术、合作伙伴与供应商、价值流与过程）。
- **官方原话**（AXELOS 官方术语专文）："The service value system in ITIL 4 now includes 34 management practices which are sets of organizational resources for performing work or accomplishing an objective."；"In the previous version of ITIL, we used the term 'processes'… In ITIL 4, the concept is expanded to include other elements – such as culture, technology and data management. Processes remain, but they don't tell the whole story."；"the chosen terminology of 'practice' means putting things into practice rather than a process to be implemented."
- **它放弃了哪两个维度**：纯过程轴（流程讲不全，故升级为"资源包"）；对象类型轴（从没按对象分卷）。它选的是**能力粒度**：一个 practice 可被多个价值流活动复用。
- URL：https://www.axelos.com/news/blogs/july-2019/translating-best-practice-and-new-itil-4-practices

### 6. Diátaxis
- **切分维度**：按**用户需求（使用场景）**。两维 = action/cognition × acquisition/application → tutorial／how-to／reference／explanation。
- **官方原话**："It prescribes approaches to content, architecture and form that emerge from a systematic approach to understanding the needs of documentation users. Diátaxis identifies four distinct needs, and four corresponding forms of documentation… and proposes that documentation should itself be organised around the structures of those needs."；"Diátaxis is based on the principle that documentation must serve the needs of its users."；"This is a *complete* map. There are only two dimensions… This is why there are necessarily four quarters to it, and there could not be three, or five. It is not an arbitrary number."
- **它明确放弃了哪个维度**（点名否掉"按对象/产品特性"）："In the absence of a clear, generalised documentation architecture, documentation creators will often try to structure their work around features of a product. This is rarely successful, even in a single instance. In a portfolio of documentation instances, the results are wild inconsistency."
- **为什么选这个维度**："it describes a **two-dimensional structure**, rather than a *list*"——列表式分类永远能被另一张列表挑战，两维才能判定。判定器是 compass 两问："action or cognition? acquisition or application?"
- **对"非文档对象"有没有说过适用范围**：**没有明说**。全站只有限定语 "Diátaxis is primarily intended as a pragmatic approach for people working on documentation."；compass 可施用于 "user situations that need documentation, or to documentation itself"。未见"可迁移到 skill/角色/节点"之类主张——把它挪到非文档对象属于外推，须自标"本地约定"。
- URL：https://diataxis.fr/ ；https://diataxis.fr/map/ ；https://diataxis.fr/foundations/ ；https://diataxis.fr/compass/ ；https://diataxis.fr/application/

### 7（补）Zachman——"按对象切"的极限形态，但它拒绝当工序
- 确实有按"对象/问句"切的体系：6 列 = What/How/Where/Who/When/Why，6 行 = 6 种视角，36 格。
- **原话**："The Zachman Framework™ IS NOT a methodology for creating the implementation (an instantiation) of the object. … The Framework (ontology) is a STRUCTURE whereas a methodology is a PROCESS. A Structure is NOT a Process."；"The Zachman Framework™ is a metamodel and unlike a methodology, does not imply anything about: Whether you do Architecture… How you do Architecture: top-down, bottom-up, left to right, right to left, where to start."
- **启示**：对象分类学与工序是两种物，业界做法是**分家**——Zachman 管"该有哪些描述"，TOGAF ADM 管"怎么产出"。想用一套卷册同时承担两者，本次未找到先例。
- URL：https://www.zachman-feac.com/zachman/about-the-zachman-framework

### 8（补）PMBOK——本轮所见唯一真矩阵，且已主动放弃
- 第 6 版：5 过程组 × 10 知识领域 = 49 个过程，每格一个过程（带 ITTO）——这就是"多对象 × 多过程"的完全矩阵。
- **第 7 版官方 FAQ 原话**："A shift in the Standard from Process Groups to principles—a set of statements that guide the actions and behaviors of project management practitioners regardless of development approach."；"A shift in the Guide from Knowledge Areas to project performance domains."；"The Process Groups and ITTOs are referenced throughout the Seventh Edition but are no longer fully included in the publication"；"the pending update avoids prescribing any specific solution because there is no one right answer for every situation."
- **为什么撤**：矩阵把"预测型方法"烧进了标准结构——轴一封闭就绑死方法论。→ **矩阵的适用条件 = 两轴都封闭 ＋ 需要穷举 ＋ 单一方法论 ＋ 面向认证**；缺一条就不该做全矩阵，应退为"主轴 ＋ 一张对照表"。
- URL：https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/pmbok-standards/pmbok-guide-public-faqs-1-july-2021.pdf

## 二、切分维度清单（本轮所见各轴，**非穷举**；带适用条件）

| 轴 | 出处 | 适用条件 | 代价 / 失效条件 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 按过程/阶段 | ISO 9001 4–10；TOGAF ADM；CMMI PA | 工作有天然时序；要认证/评估；对象会增殖 | 同一对象在多个过程被反复提及，需交叉索引 |
| 按关注点/视角 | 42010 viewpoint | 多利益相关方；需保多视图一致 | 要额外维护 concern↔view 映射；标准不给固定清单 |
| 按用户需求/使用场景 | Diátaxis 四象限 | 知识交付给"从业者"；内容形态需分家 | 不适合"要穷举检查项"的合规场景 |
| 按交付物/工作产品 | TOGAF Deliverables；CMMI typical work products | 合同签核、归档、验收 | 只能当第二轴；清单必须 informative／可替换 |
| 按成熟度/能力等级 | CMMI ML1–5 | 渐进改进、评级、路线图 | 不能单独当结构轴（内容在各级重复） |
| 按组织资源包（practice） | ITIL 4 三十四实践 | 跨职能、持续性工作、单一流程描述不全 | 边界模糊，要靠价值流串起来 |
| 按**强度/严格度**（累积包含） | buf lint 五类别：MINIMAL⊂BASIC⊂STANDARD ＋ COMMENTS／UNARY_RPC 两个正交类 | 同一对象族内的规则集要按采用程度自选；"Anything passing STANDARD also passes BASIC and MINIMAL" | 必须构成真包含关系，否则层级失效；正交类须单列，否则被误读为等级 |
| 按用途聚合的模块（vocabulary） | JSON Schema 2020-12 §4.3.3 | 规则很多、需按用途成组装载 | 单条规则不可被单独指示（见规律 6） |
| 按对象/问句分类学 | Zachman 6×6 | 完整性检查、仓储编目 | 自称不是方法，必须外挂一个工序 |
| 全矩阵（过程 × 领域） | PMBOK 6 的 5×10 | 轴封闭 ＋ 要穷举 ＋ 单一方法论 | 绑死方法论；第 7 版已撤 |

**可搬的规律（6 条）**：
1. **主结构轴选"变化最慢"的那一维**。对象类型是各组织自己定的、会增殖的（42010 把实体泛化、CMMI 22→31 PA 重组、ISO 9001 从强制程序文件到"文件化信息"）→ **不能当骨架**（能当什么、当到哪一层，见规律 5 的边界；此处不写"只能当索引"，因标识与索引是两件事）。
2. **对象清单必须出现时，业界标准做法是给它加"informative／可替换"封印**（CMMI typical work products"还有别的同样有效只是没列"、TOGAF Deliverables 可裁剪）。**但封印成立有前提：下游读者能自己补全语义。** 标准文档的下游是人（能翻页、能推断）；skill 的下游是模型（补全能力有实测上限）。同一句"这只是索引"搬过去不自动成立——索引层省 token、判据层省错误，若对照表实际承担"选哪个模块"的判断，它就是判据层，藏起来要付准确率的账（参 res-split-cost 实测：SkillRouter 8 万 skill，仅留 name+description 时 Hit@1 由 68% 降至 24%，掉 37–44pp）。→ **规则 2 只在"人读"场景免检；模型读场景须重新计价。**
3. **几乎每个标准都带一句"你不必按我的结构组织你的文件"**（ISO 9001 §0.1 最硬）——这句是防止结构反噬内容的护身符，也是"卷册结构"与"被治理内容"之间的免责边界。
4. **跨域旁证（软件架构侧），但落点要说准**：按"对象/实体"切在软件界不是没人做，而是**做了被记为反模式**——Richards & Ford 的 **entity trap**："This isn't an architecture; it's a component-relational mapping of a framework to a database."。**它的否证落点是"围绕存储而非行为组织"（内聚/行为轴），不是"粒度"**；且其失败方向是**过粗/含糊**（"boundaries too coarse to scale, test, or deploy independently"）。**不可与 grains of sand 并列**——后者是**过细**（"grains of sand"＝把每个函数/类都做成服务），两者在粒度轴上处于**相反两极**，合并引用即逻辑塌陷。→ 与我的"标准界无对象作主轴先例"是**两条独立观察**，不相加、不互相加强；能说的只是：两个领域各自独立地都没有把"对象"立成主轴，而软件侧的否定理由是"抄数据模型形状"，不是"切得太碎"。
5. **对象可以当"标识词"，不能当"结构轴"**（正面先例，非反模式）：buf lint 的规则名以 proto 元素类型为前缀（ENUM_／FIELD_／MESSAGE_／ONEOF_／RPC_／SERVICE_／PACKAGE_ 等，**绝大多数**如此；例外：PROTOVALIDATE 不守前缀、STABLE_PACKAGE_NO_IMPORT_UNSTABLE "Categories: none" 须显式启用），但**组织轴是严格度**（MINIMAL⊂BASIC⊂STANDARD ＋ COMMENTS／UNARY_RPC 两个正交类），且**交付是一个二进制**；"Rules are grouped into categories so you can opt in to a strictness level rather than enabling rules one at a time."
   **边界（防越证，队友 res-sw-eng-analog 提出并已采纳）**：buf 只证明"对象词可作**标识前缀**"，**未**证明"对象可作命名空间/目录索引"——buf 的目录结构里没有对象维度。因此"第二轴索引"不能挂 buf，须另挂载体（本轮由 TOGAF 的 Deliverables 目录、CMMI 的 work product 清单承担）。→ 我的原话"对象只能当索引"不成立，正确表述是：**"标识"与"索引"是两件事，各有各的出处，不可互相顶替**。
6. **最小可治理单位是"按用途聚合的模块"，不是最细的规则或对象**（标准正文级）：JSON Schema 2020-12 §4.3.3——"A vocabulary is generally organized around **a particular purpose**"；"While keywords can be supported outside of any vocabulary, **there is no analogous mechanism to indicate individual keyword usage**." 即单条 keyword 无法被单独指示，可指示单位只有 vocabulary。
   **且强度分三档、不对称**：模块级——未识别且标必需 → "**MUST refuse to process**"；标可选 → "**SHOULD** proceed"（§8.1.2）。规则级——"Unknown keywords **SHOULD** be treated as annotations"（§4.3.1；§6.5 同旨；§3 "collected as annotations"）。→ **硬拒绝只存在于模块层；单条规则层没有硬拒绝，只有注解降级**，且降级仍是 SHOULD 而非 MUST（规范是推荐不是强制）。这条同时给"模块按用途划界"与"能力缺失如何声明"提供了高于教材级的出处。
   **【推论·非原文，署 res-sw-eng-analog 提出、我复核逻辑】**：既然单条粒度上连"必需"都声明不出来（§4.3.3 已明说无此机制），则**凡重要到需要"不认识就拒跑"语义的判据，它就是模块级的，不该是条目级的**。反过来说，**在条目级主张"必须遵守"，在规范层面无处安放、实现方也无从拒绝**——条目级只能拥有"建议/降级"语义。此条无原文直接支撑，属逻辑推演，引用时须标"推论"。
